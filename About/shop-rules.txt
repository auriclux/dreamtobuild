======================================================================
DREAM TO BUILD: THE SHOP RULES
======================================================================

RULE #1: CALCULATE THE PACKAGING FIRST
No wedge engines in unibodies that can't support the torque. If the block 
requires cutting out structural frame rails without adding a subframe cage, 
it fails the plausibility test.

RULE #2: RESPECT THE LAWS OF AERODYNAMICS
Downforce paths must lead to real structural mount points, not sheet metal 
skin. If a wing would pull the trunk lid off at 120 mph, recalculate the 
chassis mounts.

RULE #3: OVERBUILD FOR PURPOSE
If a component is designed to handle 300 hp, build the mounting geometry for 
600 hp. If a fantasy build gets too fast or twitchy for human reaction 
times, let the robots drive.

RULE #4: DRAG PRETTY PICTURES INTO THE REAL WORLD
CAD renders and AI concepts are easy. Real metalwork is loud, hot, and slow. 
Never let a render stay a render if there is shop space and raw stock available.
