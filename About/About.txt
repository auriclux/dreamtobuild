Dream To Build is where the imagining and thinking about my custom car ideas go. They grow mushroom-like in my head, and with AI, I can now render them as photographs of things that don't exist yet, which turns out to be the best head-clearing I've found. Getting them into files cleared out the fungus; organizing them into a gallery scraped things cleaner still.

The site runs on a simple ladder. A Fantasy is a brainstorm with a chance of becoming real. Once money is committed, it becomes a Dream. When work gets done, it's a Project. The tagline (dragging high-concept pretty pictures kicking and screaming into the real world) is the colorful version of a plain truth: a render is easy; metalwork is loud, hot, slow.  And hard. The distance between them is measured in persuasion, and welds.

These are visual and mechanical confections: toys that make a smile erupt when you drive them, points of pride, heroes and villains of stories, history, a culture with different accents in Japan, Sweden, Australia, and here in SoCal. They're fun. Just look.

Nothing here is a dealer's inventory, but many things built get driven, then sold if the right offer comes. Projects will accumulate their activity and history here as the work happens.

The site itself is built and maintained with AI, the same collaborator that renders the cars. Fitting, for a garage that lives half in the imagination. And if anything here starts a bench-racing session in your head (how about a Fiat 600 with a Hayabusa engine? What driveline would you use?), then it's working as intended.
