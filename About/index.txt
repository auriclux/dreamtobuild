======================================================================
DREAM TO BUILD: ORIGIN & MANIFESTO
======================================================================

1. THE MESSAGE IN A BOTTLE
This site is built as a lark. Consider it digital driftwood tossed into the
ocean of the internet. It exists primarily because these ideas refused to 
stay inside my head, and writing HTML by hand was slowing down the eviction 
process. If this site washes up on your screen and sparks something—a weird 
chassis design, a bad idea for a shop project, or an over-engineered 
solution to a non-existent problem—then it served its purpose.

2. THE MACHINE IN THE LOOP (AI COLLABORATION)
Every pixel, line of CSS flexbox, and high-concept render on this domain is the
result of partnering with AI (mostly Gemini). 

Discovering that an AI could write the markup meant I no longer had an excuse 
to keep these concepts locked away. I use AI visual tools to extract automotive 
fantasies directly from whatever sparks them: a random name, a body line I've 
never seen before, a midnight shop conversation, or pure mechanical boredom. 

3. ARCHITECTURAL BRANCHES
The site is divided into three distinct stages of madness:

* PROJECTS (Real Builds)
  - Real-world vehicles undergoing active metalwork, fabrication, and assembly.
  - High friction, real tools, real steel, slow progress.

* DREAMS (Target Builds)
  - Achievable concepts designed with real-world physics in mind.
  - Feasible aero, suspension geometry, and engine packaging. 

* FANTASIES (Pure Vision)
  - Unconstrained visual explorations of form, proportion, and extreme style.
  - High-concept renders created to purge wild ideas from my brain.

4. THE PLAUSIBILITY RULE
Even in the deepest fantasy renders, things must make functional sense. 
No Caterpillar D7 dozer engines crammed into a classic Classic Mini—a 9-liter, 
3,000 lb industrial block will crush a 1,400 lb unibody before you can even 
tack-weld the subframe. 

Every build on this site—even the speculative ones—is evaluated for:
  - Aerodynamic feasibility and downforce paths
  - Fueling, cooling, and powertrain logic
  - Purpose-driven geometry (overbuilt for the job)

If a fantasy build turns out so absurdly fast, twitchy, or heavy that a human 
driver can't handle it, we'll let the robots drive.
======================================================================
